Exam Instructions & Rules of Conduct

Please read and acknowledge the following mandatory rules before proceeding.

Quiet Environment

Ensure you are in a completely quiet, distraction-free location. External noises or conversations may be flagged by the system.

Full-Screen Mode

Do not exit full-screen mode at any time during the assessment. Switching tabs or windows is considered a serious violation.

No Unauthorized Devices

The use of mobile phones, earphones, smartwatches, or any other technological aids for cheating is strictly prohibited. Your workspace must remain clear of all electronics.

Room Privacy

Ensure you are completely alone. No other individuals are allowed to enter the room while the exam is in progress.

Final Preparations

Complete all personal tasks before starting. You are not permitted to leave your seat once the exam begins.

AI Monitoring Active

Screen Focus

Keep your eyes focused on the screen at all times. The proctoring system continuously monitors and records suspicious head or eye movements to detect cheating behaviors.

No Virtual Machines (VM)

The exam must be taken on a physical native operating system. Running the exam within a Virtual Machine is strictly forbidden.

No Unauthorized Keystrokes

The use of automated keystrokes, macros, or unauthorized shortcuts is not allowed and will be flagged immediately.
